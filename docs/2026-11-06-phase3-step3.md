# Phase 3 step 3.3 (N=12), attempt 1 — 2026-11-06

Config: `dmic_p2=1 dmic_p3_n=12`, blob sha256 2e37e042…b9bf. Log: `logs/phase3-3-ioreg.txt`.

## Result
- Messages 1–5 accepted, error=0: PIPE_NEW, DAI COMP_NEW, DAI_CONFIG, BUFFER_NEW BUF10.2, COMP_NEW EQIIR10.0 (204 B, inline coefficients).
- **Message 6 (COMP_SET_DATA, EQ coefficients, 200 B) rejected: reply size 12, error -22 (EINVAL).** Kext aborted as designed, no retry.
- Teardown clean: final ADSPCS=0x00000f0f (idle baseline). No panic, no timeout, audio unaffected.

## Cause (found offline)
My generator used a 80-byte `sof_ipc_ctrl_data` header. The real struct is **92 bytes**:
rhdr 12 + comp_id/type/cmd/index 16 + `sof_ipc_host_buffer` **28** (include/sound/sof/stream.h:66: hdr 4 + phy_addr/pages/size 12 + reserved 12) + num_elems/elems_remaining/msg_index 12 + reserved[6] 24.
I had assumed a 16-byte host_buffer, so every field after it was shifted 12 bytes and the declared size was wrong. Same bug affects messages 9 and 10 (SET_VALUE), which were never sent.

## Fix
`tools/gen-ipc3-dmic.py`: `CTRL_HDR = 92`, host_buffer padded to 28 B, asserts on total length. Regenerated blob: 19 messages, 1584 B,
sha256 63ebf9f2…bede. Msg 6 = 212 B (92+32+88), msgs 9/10 = 108 B. Hex of msg 6 checked by hand: comp_id 52 at +0x0c, num_elems 0x58 at +0x38, abi magic 'SOF\0' at +0x5c.
Messages 1–5 are unchanged (same bytes as accepted).
